⚠ Please do not use superglue on a broken denture

Household superglues contain toxic cyanoacrylates that permanently damage prosthetic resin and distort your natural bite — making professional repair far more difficult or impossible. Keep all broken pieces dry in a container and bring them directly to our laboratory.
